Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version: this website sets no cookies and does not track you across other sites. We count visits so we know what works, we keep error reports so we can fix things, and if you give us your email we use it only to tell you about Noveniqe.

This page covers noveniqe.com, run by Noveniqe, an Amaete Venture Studios company ("we"). Each Noveniqe game and app (Agent Grand Prix, Agent Warfront, Palasora, Anansora, Jisste) has its own privacy page for what it collects when you play or chat.

What we collect here

  • Visit counts. Which pages and sections were viewed, which game links were clicked, the page you came from, your browser type and screen size. This is counted without cookies and without storing anything in your browser. Our server passes these counts on without your IP address.
  • Error reports. If something on the page breaks, the error message and the page it happened on, so we can fix it.
  • Your email, if you sign up. Only when you type it into the sign-up box and press the button.

We do not sell any of this, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not record your screen.

Who processes it

Visit counts, error reports and sign-ups are stored with PostHog, our analytics provider, on servers in the United States. The site itself is served from our own server. Fonts and scripts load from noveniqe.com, not from outside services.

How long we keep it

Visit counts and error reports are kept for up to two years. Your email is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, write to hello@noveniqe.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you live in the EU, the UK or another place with data protection law, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

You must be 13 or older to sign up (16 in EU countries that set a higher age). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their email, write to hello@noveniqe.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Big changes will also be announced on the home page.
